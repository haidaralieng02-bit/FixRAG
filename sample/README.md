# Sample manual

The repository intentionally does not ship a copyrighted manufacturer manual.

For the demo, upload a text-readable technical equipment/service manual that you are
allowed to use. A VFD, motor drive, HVAC controller, UPS, PLC, printer, or similar
manual works well.

Recommended demo question:

> The motor stops after several minutes and the drive shows an overload fault.
> What should I check first, and what are the possible causes according to the manual?

Also demonstrate an unsupported question to show FixRAG's grounding behavior.
